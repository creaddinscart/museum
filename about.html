About the collector

A single collector has been filing useful, well made and simply interesting websites
since 2009. Favour goes to sites that do one thing properly, stay online, and load quickly.

No advertising runs here, no cookies are set and nobody is tracked.
Alongside the collection there is an exhibition hall, rearranged from time to time.
